Clipper Analytics

Clipper Analytics is a private tool that shows the owner's own YouTube channel statistics: views, subscribers, watch time, click-through rate and audience retention.

It is used by one person to track their own channels. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone else.

It reads data from YouTube through the official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, using permission the channel owner grants with Google sign-in.
